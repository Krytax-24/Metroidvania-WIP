- 32rogues is not CC0. Earlier notes in this project described it as free to use without conditions; that was wrong. Its terms are above.
- `assets/` contains packed, resized copies of those packs. Ship them only as part of the game, never as a standalone asset pack, and do not commit the original zips to a public repository.
- The Ozzbit Games male hero pack in your folder is personal and non-commercial and needs a credit line. It is not used here.
- Music and sound effects are original and synthesized in the browser. There are no audio files.
- Credit line suggestion for a public build: Art by the pack authors listed above (CraftPix, GandalfHardcore, Seth Boyles, and the Knight, Blacksmith and Sprites pack creators).
